What's included

The essentials, handled properly.

Everything you need for a comfortable stay in Kampala, without the extras you don't. Here's what comes with your room.

01

24-hour front desk

Someone is always here. Late arrival, early departure, or a question at 3am, you will find us. Check-in is quick and personal, not a queue.

02

Free parking

Secure on-site parking for every guest. No charge, no reservation needed. Park when you arrive and forget about it until you leave.

03

Fitness center

A small, well-maintained gym with what you need to keep a routine going while you're away from home. Open to guests throughout the day.

04

Business center

Printing, quiet workspace, and reliable wifi. Set up a meeting or catch up on email without leaving the building.

05

Laundry service

Same-day laundry and dry-cleaning available. Leave it at the desk in the morning, it comes back folded by evening.

06

24-hour room service

Order from the restaurant menu and eat in your room, any time. Perfect for late nights or quiet mornings.

07

Concierge

Need a taxi, a restaurant booking, or directions somewhere? Just ask at the desk. We know the city and we're happy to help.

08

Free wifi

Fast, reliable, and available in every room and every public space in the hotel. No password changes, no time limits.

09

Rooftop restaurant

Breakfast, lunch, dinner, and coffee served above the city. Open to guests and to the public, all day, every day.

And what we don't have We don't have a swimming pool or a spa on the premises, and we don't pretend to. What we do have is a clean, comfortable room, attentive service, and one of the best rooftop views in Kampala.
