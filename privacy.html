Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Arcadia Suites Hotel ("we", "us", "our") is a hotel operating on Kira Road, Kampala, Uganda. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Who we are

Arcadia Suites Hotel
Kira Road
Kampala, Uganda

Email: info@arcadiasuiteshotel.com
Phone: +256 200 950 700

We are the data controller for any personal information you provide to us through this website or in the course of booking a room or dining with us.

2. What data we collect

When you submit a booking enquiry through our website, we collect:

  • Your name — so we know who is coming
  • Your email address — so we can reply to you
  • Your phone number — so we can reach you quickly
  • Check-in and check-out dates — to check availability
  • Number of guests — so we can plan the room
  • Room type preference — so we can match you with the right room
  • Any special requests you include

We do not collect payment information through this website. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

3. Why we collect it

We use your data only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your booking enquiry — this is the main reason we ask for your details
  • To provide the accommodation or dining you've requested
  • To contact you about your stay — confirming dates, rates, or changes
  • To keep basic business records as required by Ugandan law

We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted services to run our website and receive your messages:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) — stores your booking enquiry securely so we can view it in our admin panel. Provided by Google LLC. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Formspree — forwards your booking enquiry to our email inbox. See Formspree Privacy.
  • Vercel — hosts this website. See Vercel Privacy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this page.
  • Google Maps — displays the map on our location page.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. How long we keep it

Booking enquiries: kept for up to 12 months after your stay, then deleted.

Business records: kept as long as required by Ugandan tax and hotel regulations.

6. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • See what we hold about you — ask us and we'll send it
  • Correct anything wrong — tell us and we'll fix it
  • Delete your data — ask us and we'll remove it, unless we're legally required to keep it
  • Withdraw consent — for anything you previously agreed to

To exercise any of these, email info@arcadiasuiteshotel.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. How we protect your data

Your booking data is stored securely in Google Firebase with access restricted to our admin panel. Only logged-in staff can view your booking details. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections at all times.

A note on security No online system is fully secure. If you're concerned about a booking you've submitted, contact us and we'll review and delete it if needed.

8. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use tracking cookies. We don't run Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising trackers.

We do use browser local storage for one purpose only: to remember that our admin has signed in, so they don't have to log in on every page refresh. This data stays on the admin's own device and is never sent to us.

Because we don't use tracking cookies, you won't see a cookie consent banner. There's nothing to consent to.

9. Children

Our services are aimed at adults booking accommodation. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If a child has submitted data through this site, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continuing to use our site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, contact us first at info@arcadiasuiteshotel.com so we can try to fix it. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) of Uganda:

  • Website: pdpo.go.ug
  • Email: info@pdpo.go.ug
