The hotel

A quiet place to come back to.

Arcadia Suites is a small, independent hotel on Kira Road, in the heart of Kampala. We are not the biggest hotel in the city. We do not try to be. What we offer is simple: a comfortable room, friendly service, and a rooftop restaurant that guests actually want to spend time in.

Rooftop dining

Dinner with the city at your feet.

Our rooftop restaurant sits above Kira Road, close enough to Kololo to feel the evening come in. We serve buffet and international cuisine throughout the day, with a coffee menu that has quietly become a favourite among guests and neighbours.

The rooftop is also where we host meetings, corporate cocktails, private parties, and family celebrations. Tell us the occasion.

Location

In the middle of everything.

Kira Road runs through the heart of Kampala, and Arcadia Suites sits on it, minutes from Acacia Mall and the Uganda Museum.

The location suits business travellers who need to be in the city, and visitors who want to walk to shops and restaurants without needing a driver. Kololo is a short drive away.
