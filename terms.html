Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 October 2026

These are the terms under which Arcadia Suites Hotel provides accommodation, dining, and event services. They exist so both you and we know what to expect. If anything is unclear, ask us before you book and we'll explain it.

1. About us

Arcadia Suites Hotel operates from Kira Road, Kampala, Uganda.

Contact us at info@arcadiasuiteshotel.com or +256 200 950 700.

2. Booking a room

When you submit a booking enquiry through our website, by phone, or by WhatsApp, you are requesting availability and rates. No reservation is confirmed until we have confirmed it with you in writing.

A booking is confirmed when:

  • We have confirmed availability for your dates
  • We have confirmed the rate for your stay
  • You have replied to confirm you want to proceed
  • Any required deposit has been received (see below)

Until all of these are done, either side can walk away without obligation.

3. Rates and payment

Rates are quoted based on the room type, the dates, and the number of guests. Rates may change without notice until a booking is confirmed.

Payment terms

  • Payment is due on arrival, unless agreed otherwise in writing
  • We accept cash, mobile money, and major cards
  • For extended stays or event bookings, we may ask for a deposit to secure the date
Late payment If payment is not received within 14 days of the invoice date, we reserve the right to suspend further services until the balance is cleared.

4. Cancellations and changes

Plans change. If you need to cancel or move a booking, tell us as soon as you can.

If you cancel

  • More than 48 hours before check-in: No charge. Any deposit is refunded or held for a future date, whichever you prefer.
  • Less than 48 hours before check-in: We may charge the first night, or retain any deposit to cover the lost room.
  • No show: The full first-night rate may be charged.

If we cancel

If we ever need to cancel a confirmed booking, we will tell you as soon as we know and offer a full refund of any deposit paid, or an alternative room at the same rate or better. This is rare and only happens for genuine reasons.

5. Check-in and check-out

Standard check-in is from 14:00. Standard check-out is by 11:00.

Early check-in and late check-out may be available on request, subject to availability. Ask at the front desk or in advance.

Valid photo identification is required at check-in for all guests.

6. What we need from you

To make your stay comfortable, we ask that you:

  • Provide accurate information when booking, including number of guests
  • Follow reasonable hotel policies during your stay
  • Respect other guests and our team
  • Report any damages or issues as soon as they occur
  • Check out by the agreed time

7. What we are not responsible for

We take care of our guests and their belongings, but there are limits:

  • Loss or damage to valuables not deposited in the safe at reception
  • Loss or damage caused by circumstances outside our control (weather, power cuts, government action)
  • Vehicles parked on the premises (though we take reasonable care of the car park)
  • Personal items left behind after check-out (we hold these for 30 days, then dispose of them)

If you have valuable items with you, please leave them in the safe provided at reception.

8. If something goes wrong

If you are unhappy with any part of your stay, tell us while you are still with us. Front desk is open 24 hours and can usually fix things on the spot. If we can't fix it, we will discuss a fair resolution.

Our liability for any single booking is limited to the amount paid for that booking. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

9. Restaurant and events

Our rooftop restaurant is open to guests and to the public. Table reservations are recommended for evening service and can be made by phone or WhatsApp.

For private events, corporate bookings, or family celebrations, we provide a written quote and confirmation. A deposit is usually required to secure the date.

10. Conduct and safety

We ask all guests to respect the comfort of others. Excessive noise after 22:00, unlawful activity, or behaviour that endangers other guests or our team may result in a request to leave the premises without refund.

Smoking is not permitted in the rooms. Designated smoking areas are available.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. If we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. The terms that apply to your booking are the ones in place when you booked.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Uganda. Any dispute that cannot be resolved between us will be handled by the courts of Uganda.
